A Provenance

The line of descent, recorded in the manner of a catalogue entry. Blue marks a painter; the ruled line carries the name.

I

Camille Pissarro

1830–1903 · Charlotte Amalie, St. Thomas → Paris → Éragny

Painter. The only artist to exhibit in all eight Impressionist exhibitions, 1874 to 1886. Teacher of Cézanne and Gauguin.

m. Julie Vellay (1838–1926)
Their children
  • Lucien 1863–1944 · painter, printer, Eragny Press, London
  • Jeanne-Rachel, "Minette" 1865–1874
  • Georges Henri, "Manzana" 1871–1961 · painter, decorative artist
  • Félix, "Titi" 1874–1897 · painter
  • Ludovic-Rodo 1878–1952 · painter, co-author of the catalogue raisonné
  • Jeanne, "Cocotte" 1881–1948
  • Paul-Émile 1884–1972 · the youngest

II

Paul-Émile Pissarro

1884–1972 · Éragny → Clécy, Normandy

Painter. Godson of Claude Monet, who took him under his wing after Camille's death.

m. Yvonne, at whose farmhouse in Clécy the next two generations learned to paint
Their children
  • Hugues-Claude b. 1935 · the eldest
  • Yvon m. Elizabeth

III

Hugues-Claude Pissarro

b. 1935 · Neuilly-sur-Seine

Painter, signing at various times H. Claude Pissarro, Isaac Pomié, and Hugues Pissarro dit Pomié. Commissioned by the White House at 24 to paint President Eisenhower. École Normale Supérieure.

m. Katia, art dealer; later m. Corinne
Children with Katia
  • Joachim b. 1959 · the eldest
  • Lionel art dealer
  • Lélia b. 1963 · painter; raised by Paul-Émile at Clécy; m. David Stern, 1988; Stern Pissarro Gallery, London

  • Isaac
  • Rachel

IV

Joachim Pissarro

b. 1959 · Paris → New York

Art historian. Curator of Painting and Sculpture, The Museum of Modern Art, where he organized Pioneering Modern Painting: Cézanne and Pissarro (2005). Professor, Hunter College.

with Annabel Daou, conceptual artist; later m. Nataliya
The household
  • Paul-Alexander b. 1990 · son, with Annabel Daou
  • George Chorny stepbrother, through Joachim's marriage to Nataliya · filmmaker, Tisch School of the Arts
By descent to
V

Paul-Alexander Pissarro

b. 1990 · London → Hamburg

Filmmaker and conceptual artist. Founder, Unsubscribe LLC and the Church of Conceptual Art. The first of the line to make the family name itself the medium.
