One Hundred Impressionist Sunsets

RAD-GEN.001 · 2026 · with MiMo

Oil on canvas, 30.5 × 30.5 cm. Edition of one hundred. Signed P-A. Pissarro lower right, MiMo tag lower left. $7,000.

An edition of one hundred oil paintings composed strictly within the formal language of Camille Pissarro's late period, then executed by hand in heavy impasto. The edition addresses the market's historical undervaluation of Camille Pissarro relative to Monet: the serialised sunset the elder Pissarro refused to paint, painted one hundred times.

Genesis Artifact

UNSUB·GEN·001 · 2025–2026

Unlicensed Rolex Day-Date 40, archival resin, CoCA institutional seal. Edition of ten. $15,000.

The inaugural work of the Genesis series. Each edition is bound by a perpetual legal covenant compelling documented chain of custody, cash settlement, and a fifty per cent royalty to the artist on resale value exceeding $30,000. Genesis 002

UNSUB·GEN·002 · 2026

Broken iPhone, kintsugi repair with gold lacquer.

Where Genesis Artifact consecrated a counterfeit, Genesis 002 consecrated a failure: a sixteenth-century repair philosophy transposed onto disposable technology, the cracked screen treated as the site of the work rather than its flaw. The covenant structure carries forward unchanged.
