We live in our stories, and we weave them into the stories of others.

Locket Books is a bespoke memoir writing service founded by Paul-Alexander Pissarro. Through a series of unhurried sessions, our practice is one of listening deeply and helping our clients find the threads that matter. Then we set out together, weaving those threads into a lasting, personal remembrance: a record of the paths walked that reaches for narrative heights while keeping the voice of the storyteller.

We work with people who want to tell their story, see it on the page, and share it with their children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren, and, if they choose, with the public at large.

How a book is made

  1. Conversations

    Up to ten hours of recorded conversation with your parent, in person in New York or by video, at their pace.

  2. Writing

    Their words, shaped into chapters in their own voice. The family hears drafts and short recordings along the way.

  3. Photographs

    Family photographs scanned, restored and set beside the stories they belong to.

  4. The book

    A linen hardcover of 120 to 150 pages, five copies, and the complete audio recordings, so the voice is kept too.

Questions families ask

My parent isn't much of a talker. Will this work?

Most people are not storytellers at first. The sessions are built for that: gentle questions, old photographs and time. The stories come once someone is listening.

We don't live in New York.

Sessions can take place by video, wherever your parent lives. Every book is delivered to your door.

Who sees the book?

Your family owns the finished book and the recordings. Nothing is shared or published unless you choose to share it.

How does payment work?

Commissions start at $15,000. Half the fee on signing reserves the commission and starts the interviews within seven days. The rest is due when the books are in your hands.

Can it be a gift?

Yes. Families who sign by 30 November receive a printed gift announcement to give their parent over the holidays. The book follows.
