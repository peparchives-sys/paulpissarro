Camille Pissarro's great-great-grandson is making new Pissarros, with AI and by hand

One Hundred Impressionist Sunsets: an edition of one hundred oil paintings in the late style of Camille Pissarro.

Press kit · October 2026 · Download as PDF · paul@paulpissarro.com

The story

One Hundred Impressionist Sunsets (RAD-GEN.001, 2026) is an edition of one hundred oil paintings, each 30.5 by 30.5 cm, issued by the Church of Conceptual Art. A generative system was instructed to compose strictly within the formal language of Camille Pissarro's late period; each resulting study is then painted by hand, in oil, by the painter Kim Van Do. It is the first work in the RAD-GEN series, short for Radical Genealogy; the project's statement of method is "the lineage is the medium."

The edition addresses the market's historical undervaluation of Camille Pissarro relative to Claude Monet, which the artist attributes to his ancestor's refusal of the single-subject serialisation that produced Monet's Haystacks, Rouen Cathedrals and Water Lilies. The project in one line: the serialised sunset the elder Pissarro refused to paint, painted one hundred times.

Each work is issued at $7,000 through Name of My Gallery and bound by a perpetual covenant: documented chain of custody, cash settlement, and a fifty per cent royalty to the artist on any resale value above $14,000. The covenant is the artwork's legal skeleton, drafted to bind the object across all future transfers.

Paul-Alexander Pissarro is a conceptual artist in London and the sixth generation of working Pissarro artists and scholars. The edition is produced under his direction with Wolf Mike Mozart (MiMo); Jesse Do is technical supervisor.

For a deep dive, the project site shows the edition painting by painting, each marked by stage from pre-study to finished, and takes reservations: rad-gen001.unsubscribe.llc
