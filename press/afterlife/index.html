Heaven costs $777. Hell costs $666.

The Church of Conceptual Art has opened ticketing for the afterlife.

Press kit · October 2026 · Download as PDF · paul@paulpissarro.com

The story

In July 2026 the Church of Conceptual Art opened ticketing for the afterlife. A Ticket to Heaven (H-777) lists at $777. A Ticket to Hell (D-666) lists at $666. Both Gates (HD-1443) lists at $1,443. Passes are named, non-transferable and never expire, and can be bought for oneself, as a gift, or as unnamed bearer passes.

Payment is the only qualification; there is no review of conduct. Every sale is recorded in a public, append-only ledger built on a SHA-256 hash chain that anyone can recompute. The passes are distributed as conceptual art objects and confer no rights or services in this life. They cannot be cancelled, amended or refunded.

From the announcement: "Every religion in history has sold this product," said Paul-Alexander Pissarro, co-founder of the Church of Conceptual Art. "We are simply the first to publish the price, the terms, and the ledger." The church, he added, "asks you to be a customer, which the record suggests is the more honest relationship."

The program is the church's first sacrament available at retail, issued through Unsubscribe NYC LLC, with tickets at finaldues.com. Gate intake, processing speed and audits remain at the discretion of the afterlife administrations, which did not respond to press inquiries.
