about_paul-alexander.html

A winding path is still a path.

I am Paul-Alexander Pissarro, a coach and artist based in London. I am French-American and the great-great-grandson of impressionist painter Camille Pissarro. I am here because of the artists and thinkers before me.

I trained as a chef in Paris, made films, produced conceptual art, got sober, and have maintained a daily Buddhist practice for over a decade. I have lost things I cared about and rebuilt from wherever I landed. None of it went according to plan, yet I wouldn't trade any of it.

I don't bring a rigid formula to our sessions. I bring a practice of total honesty (even when inconvenient) and years of helping others do the same. Most people I work with stand at a critical threshold in their creative work, their sobriety, or somewhere harder to define.

readme.txt

What this is (and what it isn't)

  • It is not therapy. If you need therapy, I will gladly help you find a qualified professional.
  • It is not a rigid program with generic worksheets or a certificate at the end. We adapt the process in real time.

It is an honest conversation between two people. One who has navigated deep transitions and shares insights when useful. The other is you.

Most of the time, that is enough to break down walls.
