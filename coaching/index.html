About Paul-Alexander

A winding path
is still a path.

I am Paul-Alexander Pissarro, a coach and creative based in Hamburg, Germany. I am French-American, the great-great-grandson of Camille Pissarro, and I have spent most of my adult life figuring out what it means to build something honest from a complicated inheritance.

I have been a chef. A filmmaker. A conceptual artist. A person in recovery. A student of Buddhist practice for over a decade. I have lost things that mattered deeply and rebuilt from wherever I happened to land. None of it followed a plan and I would not trade any of it.

What I bring to a session is not a system. It is the accumulated honesty of someone who has had to tell himself the truth in hard moments, and learned to help others do the same. I work with people at turning points — in their creative lives, in their relationship with sobriety, in the quiet places where the real questions live.

I want to be honest with you about what coaching with me is and is not.

It is not therapy. If you need therapy, please find a good therapist. I will help you look.

It is not a program with a fixed outcome and a timeline and a certificate at the end. I do not know exactly where we are going. That is the point.

What it is: a conversation between two people. One of them has been through some things and is willing to say so. The other is you, willing to show up honestly for thirty minutes and see what happens.

Most of the time, that is enough to change something.
