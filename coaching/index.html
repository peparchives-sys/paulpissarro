About Paul-Alexander

A winding path
is still a path.

I am Paul-Alexander Pissarro, a coach and artist based in London. I am French-American and the great-great-grandson of Camille Pissarro. A famous name is a strange thing to inherit, and working out what to do with mine has taken most of my adult life.

I trained as a chef in Paris, made films, made conceptual art, got sober, and have practised Buddhism every day for more than ten years. I have lost things I cared about and started again from wherever I landed. None of it went to plan, and I would keep all of it.

I don't bring a system to a session. I bring the habit of telling myself the truth when it was inconvenient, and some practice at helping other people do the same. Most of the people I work with are at a turning point, in their work, in their sobriety, or somewhere harder to name.

Here is what coaching with me is, and what it isn't.

It is not therapy. If you need therapy, please find a good therapist. I will help you look.

It is not a program with a timeline and a certificate at the end. I won't know where we are going before we start, and neither will you.

It is a conversation between two people. One of them has been through a few things and will tell you about them when it helps. The other is you.

Most of the time, that is enough to change something.
